ABOUT US

Welcome to BRT FLOWERS. Our journey began with a simple belief: flowers shouldn't just sit in a vase; they should tell a story. Founded by VB SHILENGE, we stepped away from mass-produced commercial bundles to focus on bespoke, organically styled arrangements that celebrate nature’s natural movement.We source our blooms daily from ethical, sustainable growers to ensure your flowers arrive in pristine, long-lasting condition. Every bouquet is custom-designed in our private studio and hand-delivered straight to your door. Whether you are celebrating a romance, sending comfort, or treating yourself, we treat every order as our most important arrangement.
